# Artemis Construction Intelligence

## Decision

Construction is the first and most developed Artemis 2 industry vertical. It has a dedicated information architecture, product catalog, lead funnel, analytics namespace, editorial voice, and demonstration workflow.

Artemis remains the parent operating organization. Construction is not mixed into marine, heritage, creative, or general research sales journeys.

## Navigation boundary

The main Artemis navigation routes construction clients directly into `/construction`. Once inside the vertical, the local navigation excludes unrelated Artemis products except for a restrained parent-brand link.

## Editorial standard

Construction pages must be institutional, technical, precise, evidence-led, and contractor-oriented. Avoid vague AI claims, generic startup language, unrelated experiments, and excessive futuristic imagery.

Every product page must disclose:

- product status: Live, Pilot, Demonstration, Research, or Planned;
- intended users;
- supported geography and data sources;
- current limitations;
- security and privacy posture;
- pricing or pilot path;
- last verified date.

## Lead routing

Construction inquiries are classified by company type, role, geography, project market, primary challenge, product interest, current systems, and implementation horizon. They must not enter an unclassified generic contact queue.
