Cost Scenarios
What net income looks like under different cost-of-goods assumptions
Why the app shows $0.00 cost & net income of $15,108.30: No supplier invoices have been recorded yet, so Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) is $0. That means the figure labeled "Est. Net Income" is really gross profit after fees & overhead — not true net income. It will drop as you record real product costs (via Vendors & AP, or by categorizing TD Bank supplier payments as COGS). The scenarios below bracket the possibilities.
The Governing Formula i

Net Income (all scenarios use this)

$$\text{Net} = \underbrace{(\text{Revenue} + \text{Shipping}_{\text{collected}})}_{\text{Gross}} - \text{Discounts} - \text{Fees} - \text{Overhead} - \boxed{\text{COGS}} - \text{OpEx}$$

Only COGS changes between scenarios. With your data: Gross $27,260.05 − Discounts $10,483 − Merchant fees $873.58 − Overhead $795.17 = $15,108.30 available before COGS.

Scenario 1 — Cost = Sale Price

$$\text{COGS} = \text{Subtotal} = \$27{,}200.05 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{Net} = -\$12{,}091.75$$

Assumes you paid your supplier exactly what the customer paid you. Zero product margin; the loss equals all fees + overhead.

Scenario 2 — Break-even

$$\text{COGS}_{\text{BE}} = \text{Net}_{\text{after fees \& overhead}} = \$15{,}108.30 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{Net} = \$0$$

The exact product cost at which the operation breaks even. Any real cost above this is a loss; below it is profit.

Scenario 3 — Loss = Shipping Cost

$$\text{COGS} = \text{COGS}_{\text{BE}} + \text{Shipping}_{\text{absorbed}} = \$15{,}108.30 + \$10{,}483.00 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{Net} = -\$10{,}483.00$$

Break-even cost plus the shipping the business absorbs on free-delivery orders. The operation runs at a loss exactly equal to that absorbed shipping.

🚀 Quick Start
  1. Dashboard — your live KPIs (revenue, discounts, fees, overhead, net income). Click i icons anywhere for an explanation of any module.
  2. Sync Squarespace — top bar ⟳ button pulls the latest orders on demand (also auto-syncs every 4 hours).
  3. Record real costs — Vendors & AP → + Vendor Invoice → enter products, quantities & unit costs. This turns the amber "pre-COGS" net income into green "actual" net income and powers Product Margins.
  4. Reconcile the bank — TD Bank tab: categorize transactions and mark them reconciled; categorized costs post to the ledger automatically.
  5. File taxes — Tax & Compliance (NY sales tax) and Federal Tax Forms (W-2 / 1099 generation with audit packages).
  6. Export & print — Invoices tab → 👁 View / ⬇ download any customer invoice PDF; most tables have CSV export.

Revenue Recognition

Gross Revenue

$$\text{Gross Revenue} = \sum(\text{Product Subtotal}) + \sum(\text{Shipping Collected})$$

Product subtotal is the sum of all line items. Shipping collected is the carrier rate net of any discount that fully or partially offsets it. Free-shipping promotions zero out shipping collected.

Shipping Collected (Free Shipping Accounting)

$$\text{Shipping Collected} = \max\!\left(0,\ \text{Shipping Listed} - \text{Discount Total}\right)$$

Squarespace reports shippingTotal as the carrier rate before promotion. When a "Free Delivery" discount fully covers it, discountTotal equals shippingTotal, resulting in $0 collected and the full amount posted as an absorbed discount (DISCOUNT debit in the ledger).

Discounts Absorbed

$$\text{Discount Absorbed} = \sum\left(\text{Shipping Listed} - \text{Shipping Collected}\right)$$

Represents the cost of free-shipping and promotional offers borne by Prime Industrial. Posted as a DISCOUNT debit — reduces gross to net revenue.

Profitability Waterfall

Merchant Processing Fee

$$\text{Merchant Fee} = \text{Grand Total} \times \frac{r}{100} + f$$

Where \(r\) = fee rate (default 2.9%) and \(f\) = fixed per-transaction fee (default $0.30). Applied to grand total (including tax) as the payment processor charges on the full amount collected.

Net After Fees

$$\text{Net After Fees} = \text{Gross Revenue} - \text{Discounts} - \text{Merchant Fees} - \text{Platform Fees}$$

This is the effective cash position before overhead and cost of goods. It is the base for the 5% overhead allocation.

Overhead Allocation (5%)

$$\text{Overhead} = \text{Net After Fees} \times 0.05$$

Applied after fees and discounts, not on gross revenue. Covers utilities, packaging materials, incidental labor, insurance allocations, and other operating costs not tracked as direct expenses.

Estimated Net Income

$$\text{Net Income} = \text{Net After Fees} - \text{Overhead} - \text{COGS} - \text{OpEx} - \text{Other Expenses}$$

Management estimate before personal income tax. Sales tax (\(\text{NY} = 8.875\%\)) is excluded — it is a pass-through liability collected from customers and remitted to New York State quarterly via ST-100.

COGS & Supplier Invoice Placeholder

Estimated COGS Placeholder

$$\text{Est. COGS} = \text{Grand Total} - \text{Sales Tax} - \text{Merchant Fee}$$

Used as a temporary placeholder when actual supplier invoices have not yet been entered. Represents the net proceeds from an order (what we keep before paying the supplier). Once real vendor invoices are posted in Vendors & AP, the placeholder is replaced by the actual COGS figure.

Gross Margin

$$\text{Gross Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Gross Revenue} - \text{COGS}}{\text{Gross Revenue}} \times 100$$

Target gross margin varies by product category. Industrial supply typically targets 20–40%. Below 15% should trigger a supplier price renegotiation review.

Line-Item COGS & Product Margins i

Line Item → Ledger

$$\text{Goods}_{\text{line}} = \text{Qty} \times \text{Unit Cost} \;\rightarrow\; \textbf{COGS (debit)}$$ $$\text{Extras}_{\text{line}} = \text{Tax} + \text{Shipping} + \text{Fees} \;\rightarrow\; \textbf{EXPENSE (debit)}$$

Each vendor invoice line splits into two ledger postings: the pure goods cost hits COGS, while inbound tax, freight and fees hit EXPENSE. Both reduce net income. Entries are tagged VI:<id> so re-saving never double-counts.

Reconciled Net Income

$$\text{Net}_{\text{actual}} = \text{Net}_{\text{pre-COGS}} - \text{COGS} - \text{Expenses}$$

The dashboard KPI shows pre-COGS (amber) until any vendor invoice is posted, then switches to reconciled actual (green). The Cost Scenarios tab's first card mirrors this.

Per-Product Margin

$$\text{Margin}_{\text{product}} = \underbrace{\sum \text{Revenue}_{\text{Squarespace}}}_{\text{by product name}} - \underbrace{\sum (\text{Qty} \times \text{Unit Cost})}_{\text{vendor lines}}$$

Revenue (from Squarespace order line items) and cost (from vendor invoice lines) are matched on product name (case-insensitive). Categories are assigned on the cost side and roll up in the Product Margins tab. A product with sales but no recorded cost shows 100% margin — a flag to enter its supplier cost.

NY Sales Tax

NY Combined Sales Tax Rate

$$\text{Tax Rate} = \underbrace{4.0\%}_{\text{NY State}} + \underbrace{4.5\%}_{\text{NYC}} + \underbrace{0.375\%}_{\text{MCTD}} = 8.875\%$$

MCTD = Metropolitan Commuter Transportation District surcharge. Applies to sales within New York City (Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx, Staten Island). Filed quarterly on Form ST-100.

Tax Collected from Customer

$$\text{Tax} = \text{Subtotal} \times 0.08875$$

Tax is a pass-through — collected from the customer and held until remittance to New York State. It is a current liability on the balance sheet, not income.

Invoice Number Formula

Deterministic Invoice Number

$$\text{Invoice \#} = \texttt{112-}\underbrace{H_1[0..6]}_{\text{7 chars}}\texttt{-}\underbrace{H_1[7..14]}_{\text{8 chars}}$$

Where \(H_1 = \text{MD5}(\text{Squarespace Order ID})\). The prefix "112" mirrors an Amazon-style format. Using a hash of the order ID makes invoice numbers deterministic — re-running the emailer produces the same number every time, preventing duplicate invoices.

Glossary
Accounting & finance terms used in this ERP system
Accounts Payable (AP)
Money owed by Prime Industrial to suppliers and vendors for goods or services received but not yet paid. Appears as a current liability on the balance sheet. Tracked in the Vendors & AP tab.
Chart of Accounts
The complete list of ledger accounts used in this system: REVENUE, SHIPPING, DISCOUNT, TAX, MERCHANT_FEE, COGS, EXPENSE. Each transaction is posted to one or more of these accounts using double-entry bookkeeping.
COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)
The direct cost of the merchandise sold to customers — the supplier's invoice amount. Reduces gross profit. Posted as a debit to the COGS account when a vendor invoice is entered. Before actual invoices are entered, an estimated placeholder is calculated as Grand Total − Tax − Merchant Fee.
Credit
In double-entry bookkeeping, a credit increases liability, equity, and revenue accounts, and decreases asset and expense accounts. Revenue (sales), tax collected, and shipping collected are posted as credits.
Debit
In double-entry bookkeeping, a debit increases asset and expense accounts, and decreases liability and revenue accounts. Discounts absorbed, merchant fees, COGS, and expenses are posted as debits.
Discount Absorbed
The cost of promotional offers borne by the business — primarily free shipping. When a customer receives free delivery, the shipping cost that Prime Industrial covers is posted as a DISCOUNT debit in the ledger. This reduces net revenue.
Double-Entry Bookkeeping
An accounting method where every transaction affects at least two accounts: a debit to one and a credit to another, always keeping the ledger in balance (Assets = Liabilities + Equity).
General Ledger
The master record of all financial transactions. Every order sync and vendor invoice posts entries here. The ledger is the source of truth for all P&L calculations in this system.
Grand Total
The total amount charged to the customer: Subtotal + Shipping Collected + Tax. This is the amount the payment processor (e.g., Stripe) charges the customer's card, and is the basis for the merchant fee calculation.
Gross Revenue
Total product sales plus shipping collected (before any deductions). Also called "top line." In this system: Product Subtotal + Net Shipping Collected.
Merchant Fee
The processing fee charged by the payment processor (e.g., Stripe via Squarespace) for each transaction. Default rate: 2.9% of grand total + $0.30 per transaction. Posted as a MERCHANT_FEE debit. Configurable in Assumptions.
Net Income
The bottom-line profitability estimate: Gross Revenue − Discounts − Merchant Fees − Overhead − COGS − OpEx − Other Expenses. This is a management estimate, not a tax filing figure. Personal income tax is not deducted here.
OpEx (Operating Expenses)
General business expenses not directly tied to a specific sale. In this system, estimated as a percentage of gross revenue (default 10%). Includes software subscriptions, office supplies, shipping materials, and other overhead costs tracked as a percentage allocation until actual expenses are posted.
Overhead Allocation
A 5% charge applied to Net After Fees (after discounts and merchant fees). Covers utilities, packaging, incidental labor, insurance, and miscellaneous operating costs. Applied after fees because these costs are partially volume-driven. Configurable as overhead_pct in Assumptions.
Reconciliation
The process of matching expected cash deposits (Revenue + Shipping − Discounts − Merchant Fees) against actual bank activity (checks issued, ACH payments, etc.) to ensure the books agree with the bank statement.
Sales Tax (NY ST-100)
New York State combined sales tax of 8.875% (4% state + 4.5% NYC + 0.375% MCTD) collected from customers on taxable sales. Filed quarterly using Form ST-100 on the NY State Tax portal. A current liability — not income.
Shipping Listed vs. Shipping Collected
Shipping Listed: the carrier rate shown before discounts (what Squarespace calls shippingTotal). Shipping Collected: what the customer actually paid, after promotion offsets. If free shipping was applied, Shipping Collected = $0 and the full listed amount is posted as a Discount Absorbed.
Squarespace Commerce API
The API (v1) used to sync order data from Prime Industrial's Squarespace store at greatorder.org. Authenticated with a Bearer token. Orders are polled every 4 hours by the invoice emailer and on-demand via the Sync button. Endpoint: GET /1.0/commerce/orders.
Subtotal
The sum of all product line items before tax and shipping. Used as the revenue credit in the general ledger and as the basis for sales tax calculation.
WAL Mode (SQLite)
Write-Ahead Logging — a SQLite journal mode that allows concurrent reads while a write is in progress. Enabled in this system to prevent "database is locked" errors when the Flask server and invoice emailer access the database simultaneously.
