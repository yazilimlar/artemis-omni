# AttemptBook Logo — Canonical Artwork

Owner's design (Gokmen, 2026-11-03): an open book. The left page carries the
letter **A**; the right page carries a **3 × 5 grid of checkboxes — 15 in
total, 13 checked and 2 blank** (the final two, bottom row). The ledger is
almost complete; two attempts are still open.

## Palette

- Cover: navy `#0B1F3B` (light variant) / gold `#D4A017` (dark variant)
- Pages: cream `#FBF7EA`, navy outline; the letter A in navy, Georgia serif,
  with a gold rule beneath
- Checkboxes: navy outline, white fill; check marks in dark gold `#B98A0E`

## Placement

- Family-bar mini on all three family pages (artloop, artplex,
  ai-operations-intelligence): the full mark at 30 × 23 — no separate
  simplification yet; if it ever reads poorly at small size, a simplified
  mini is cut from this file, never redrawn freehand.
- Family card on the ArtLoop and ArtPlex pages: `attemptbook-plate.svg`.
