# Artemis Omni — Product Vision

## The thesis

Complex engineering and construction programs generate an enormous volume of technical
artifacts — cost curves, schedules, BIM fragments, maps, dashboards, invoices,
project-control reports. Most of that value never reaches the people making decisions,
because turning artifacts into insight is slow, manual, and inconsistent.

**Artemis Omni closes that gap** with an AI-first intelligence layer that composes
artifacts into executive-grade decisions — with the craft of a classical engineering
atelier and the speed of modern automation.

## Who it serves

- **Project controls & PMO** — cost/schedule integration, EAC, cash flow
- **Engineering** — BIM and geotechnical signals tied to the model of record
- **Operations & finance** — invoice automation, escalation clauses, reporting
- **Executives** — board-ready dashboards and narratives on demand

## Product surfaces

1. **Solutions** — productized AI systems per domain
2. **Labs** — experimental cinematic intelligence and prototypes
3. **Academy** — tutorials, glossaries, frameworks (AI-generated content packages)
4. **Tools** — interactive calculators and dashboards
5. **Case Studies** — measured outcomes

## What makes it different

- **Accuracy over novelty** — every output is traceable to source artifacts.
- **Auditable automation** — agents flag exceptions, they don't hide them.
- **Editorial craft** — outputs are legible and elegant, not generic SaaS dashboards.

## Phasing

- **Phase 1 (this repo):** marketing + content foundation, sample tool, cinematic hero.
- **Phase 2:** live contact/pilot backend, more tools, real infographics, first
  authenticated dashboard (Supabase) — *only when explicitly requested*.
- **Phase 3:** polished cinematic scenes (Draco GLB / R3F), case-study data, Academy at
  scale via the content-automation pipeline.

## Success criteria for Phase 1

Fast first load, all routes present, premium identity, working cinematic prototype +
fallback, mobile + reduced-motion support, no committed secrets, and a structure that
lets future agents safely add content, tools, and scenes.
