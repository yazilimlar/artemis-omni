# Artemis Omni — Brand Architecture

> **Internal strategy document.** This describes the full long-term architecture.
> **Do NOT expose the broader SMB / Business OS positioning on the public homepage yet.**
> The public site leads with the construction/infrastructure beachhead first (see the
> roadmap rule at the bottom).

## Company umbrella

**ARTEMIS OMNI** — an AI implementation, automation, and intelligence systems company.

The umbrella spans three layers: a near-term construction beachhead, a longer-term
horizontal platform of "Core" modules, and a set of industry verticals. Public messaging
unlocks these layers in sequence as credibility compounds.

## Layer 1 — Near-term beachhead (public NOW)

**5D Construction Intelligence Bridge** for heavy civil, infrastructure, project
controls, ERP/CMiC workflows, digital twins, forecasting, and executive action.

This is the *only* positioning the public homepage should lead with today. It is
specific, credible, and defensible:

> Artemis connects design, geometry, quantities, schedule, field production, actual
> cost, billing revenue, PM Forecast, system-generated projections, cashflow,
> risk/opportunity, and executive action.

Audience: heavy civil contractors, infrastructure owners, PMCM teams, estimators,
project controls managers, and executives.

## Layer 2 — Longer-term horizontal platform (NOT public yet)

**Artemis Core** modules — a horizontal AI Business OS for small and mid-sized
businesses:

| Module | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| **Artemis Flow** | Finance, banking, cashflow, AI CFO-style analytics |
| **Artemis Desk** | Customer support, sales assistant, communications automation |
| **Artemis Docs** | Document intelligence — contracts, bids, unstructured data extraction |
| **Artemis Connect** | Connectors — Gmail, Drive, QuickBooks, Shopify, ERP, banking |
| **Artemis Ops** | Operating dashboards, DuckDB-style analytics, business decision systems |

## Layer 3 — Industry verticals (NOT public yet, except Construct framing)

| Vertical | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| **Artemis Construct** | Heavy civil cost control, PM Forecast, ERP/CMiC, construction analytics |
| **Artemis Twin** | 3D/4D/5D digital twin and engineering visualization |
| **Artemis Atlas** | Geospatial, map intelligence, infrastructure / historical / cultural mapping |

> The public "5D Construction Intelligence Bridge" *is* the outward face of **Artemis
> Construct** (+ Twin for the model dimensions). The other modules/verticals stay in
> strategy docs until the construction positioning is credible.

## Naming guardrails (public copy)

- ✅ Use: "5D Construction Intelligence Bridge", "Project Intelligence Command Center",
  "Cashflow Intelligence Engine", "System-Generated Projections", "Forecast Variance
  Engine".
- ❌ Avoid on the public site: "Atelier" and artistic/mystical framing; generic AI/SaaS
  clichés ("decision mesh", "AI-first command room"); broad SMB Business OS claims;
  unsupported promises (e.g. "we'll follow up within 24 hours"); vague 6D/7D/8D.
- There is **no serious "1D"** product layer.

## Roadmap rule

The public website leads with the construction/infrastructure beachhead first. The
broader SMB AI Business OS vision (Core modules, additional verticals) is introduced
**gradually**, only after the 5D construction positioning is credible. See
`ProductRoadmap.md` for sequencing.
