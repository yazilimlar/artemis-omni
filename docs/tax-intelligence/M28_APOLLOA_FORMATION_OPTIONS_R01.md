# Apolloa Formation Options — Preliminary Decision Brief R01

**Date:** 2026-10-26. **Status:** DRAFT_ASSUMPTION; NO FORMATION AUTHORIZED.
**Purpose:** Compare organizational/legal options for independent AgoraXAI participant Apolloa, which owner states is not yet registered, with prospective food/beverage import-export, consulting and apparel design.

## Options
| Model | Advantage | Material concern | Preliminary fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| NY domestic LLC | Direct operating-state structure if NY-centered; independent legal person | NY formation, operating agreement, newspaper publication, tax and regulatory registrations | Baseline |
| DE LLC + NY foreign qualification | Flexible governance, investment familiarity | DE annual tax $300, registered agent, NY qualification and NY publication if doing business there | Conditional |
| NJ LLC | Fit for actual NJ operations | NJ formation/reporting plus NY qualification if doing business NY | Conditional |
| Great Order LLC operating Apolloa trade name | Fewer new entities initially | No separate Apolloa liability shield; operator bears obligations; trade-name registration and contracts must identify actual party | Transitional only after verifying Great Order legal/tax status |
| Remain pre-registration | No premature formation | Cannot claim incorporated status; no regulated trading without correct legal operator and permits | Recommended current planning state |

## Material regulatory gates
1. Food import: identify product/SKU, source country, importer of record, customs classification, FDA prior notice, facility registration, FSVP applicability, labeling, any commodity-specific controls; no assumption that forming LLC confers permission.
2. NY taxable sales: determine product/service taxability and obtain Certificate of Authority where required at least 20 days before business requiring registration.
3. Apparel: identify labeling, country of origin, product safety and customs requirements.
4. Consulting: contracts, scope, professional licensure where applicable.
5. Ownership: members, manager authority, capital contributions, IP ownership, platform terms and shared services separately evidenced.
6. Liability: food/product liability insurance, contractual risk allocation, customs broker, accounting controls.

## Recommended sequencing
**Phase A:** no filing; record Apolloa as independent agora / pre-registration initiative. Gather non-confidential assumptions: intended operating state, initial product category, domestic vs import, first customers, expected owners, investor intent.
**Phase B:** counsel/CPA compare NY LLC vs Great Order trade name vs DE/NJ using actual facts; estimate 3-year total costs, tax/insurance exposure, cross-border and foreign-qualification duties.
**Phase C:** owner chooses entity, jurisdiction, equity and legal operator; only then authorize filings and original-document intake under security gates.
