## 6. Cross-cutting regulatory gates (every option, including DBA and wait)

Formation never confers trading permission (each gate is per-product / per-activity, with its own actor and evidence):

1. **Customs:** importer of record is a CBP role; broker/bond arrangements are separate from FDA and from formation.
2. **FDA food import:** FDA does not license/approve importers. Prerequisites sit elsewhere: facility registration for each facility that manufactures/processes/packs/holds, per-shipment prior notice, shipment inspection/detention exposure.
3. **FSVP:** the FSVP importer is the U.S. owner/consignee at entry (21 CFR 1.500), with a per-food + per-supplier program (hazard analysis, evaluation, verification, corrective actions). It is a named person's program, not a property of "the LLC".
4. **NY taxable sales:** Certificate of Authority is activity-based (TB-ST-175), ≥20 days before; a pre-formation operator can already be a required vendor; marketplace-provider vs marketplace-seller analysis is required if Apolloa sells through AgoraXAI or another platform. Collected tax is a **liability held for the state** — never revenue, never commingled, remitter pathway stays **U1-UNKNOWN** until processor evidence distinguishes it.
5. **Apparel:** labelling, country-of-origin, product-safety, customs per product (no verified figure exists; gates only).
6. **Consulting:** written scope/contracts; licensure where the actual service requires it.
7. **Employer duties:** NY/NJ withholding and employer registration if employees in either state.
8. **Insurance:** food/product liability in the **actual operator's** name, expressly covering the Apolloa activities/products — written insurer confirmation required.

**Food-import readiness is identical under all options: not ready until these gates are evidenced per product. Entity choice selects *who* the operator is; it does not advance readiness.**

## 7. Option 4 — Great Order trade name (DBA) and Option 5 — Wait

**Option 4 — DBA.** Fork on the operator's verified legal form: if Great Order is an LLC → DOS Certificate of Assumed Name **$25**, no county fee; if the operator were an individual/partnership → county-clerk certificates per county, county-set fees. **Because Great Order's form is U2-UNKNOWN, this option is NOT COSTABLE today** and cannot advance past "transitional, after verification". Structural facts that no filing changes: an assumed name creates **no** separate legal person, shield, EIN, or tax classification; Apolloa liabilities would be the operator's liabilities (cross-exposure to all operator assets, and vice versa); contracts, invoices, bank, customs and tax registrations must name the real legal party (e.g. "[Great Order LLC] d/b/a Apolloa" — only once operator + assumed-name filing are evidenced); IP filed in this model belongs to the operator, not to "Apolloa". Precondition (not optional): verified Great Order formation, good standing, operating-agreement authority, and tax classification — all currently MISSING from evidence.

**Option 5 — Remain pre-registration (wait).** $0 formation fees — but **not $0-risk**: no shield, no Apolloa contracting/importing/insuring capacity in its own name, and any Apolloa-branded activity still requires a named actual legal operator with its own registrations (see gates document). Benefits: no premature filing, no publication/annual-tax clock started, no wrong-form filing to unwind.
