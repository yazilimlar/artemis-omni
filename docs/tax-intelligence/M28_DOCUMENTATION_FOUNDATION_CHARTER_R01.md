# M2.8 Documentation Foundation — Charter v0.1
Status: WORKING DRAFT / SYNTHETIC ONLY. Not an executed agreement, tax advice, ownership determination, or production authorization.
## Objective
Create a complete, versioned, evidence-linked, privacy-preserving documentary foundation before further M2.8 runtime integration. All relationships must be configurable, time-bounded, revocable, and traceable. Do not hard-code provisional ownership, account linkage, tax treatment, or legal classifications.
## Scope / workstreams
1. Corporate and governance: legal entity registry, trade names/brands/projects, resolutions, delegated authority, ownership decision log.
2. Legal and commercial: client/vendor agreements, statement of work, service-level terms, intercompany/shared-services templates, IP/open-source licenses, privacy and data-processing clauses.
3. Finance and tax: chart of accounts, cost centers, accruals, invoicing, receipts, payment matching, sales tax withholding/remittance, W-2/1099 intake, quarterly/year-end checklist, tax return document requests.
4. Treasury and operations: processor/bank account inventory, authorization/mandates, payout settlement statements, reconciliations, supplier and client master data, outstanding obligations.
5. Technology: source systems, connector consent, RLS/tenant and storage boundaries, secrets management, audit logs, data retention and document access.
6. Evidence and compliance: document request list, received files inventory, evidence quality and source provenance, effective dates, retention, signature and independent verification gates.
