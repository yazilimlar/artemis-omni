## Draft agreement catalog
Internal: brand/IP use, shared services, cost allocation, intercompany billing (only where distinct legal parties exist), delegated authority, conflict of interest.
External: client MSA/SOW, vendor/supplier terms, NDA, DPA, software support/hosting, open-source licensing notice, contractor IP assignment.
All are unexecuted templates pending legal review; avoid representing an intra-entity transfer as an intercompany agreement.
## Decision dependencies
U1 processor withholding/remittance: processor source reports and tax remittance evidence.
U2 entity ownership: formation/operating/IP and registration documents, owner confirmation.
U3 tax treatment: jurisdiction, actual transactions, entity classification, CPA review.
U4 history/privacy: scoped repository exposure inventory, data sensitivity assessment, owner-approved remediation.
