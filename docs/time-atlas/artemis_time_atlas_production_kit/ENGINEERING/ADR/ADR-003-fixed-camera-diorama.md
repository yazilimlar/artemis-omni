# ADR-003 — Fixed Camera Isometric Diorama

## Status
Accepted

## Decision
V1 will use a fixed cinematic isometric/axonometric camera with subtle auto-pan and pointer parallax.

## Rationale
A fixed camera gives stronger art direction, better mobile performance, less implementation complexity, and a more premium scroll narrative.

## Consequences
- No free orbit controls in production.
- Camera motion is scripted and bounded.
- The scene is composed like a poster or museum model, not an open world.
