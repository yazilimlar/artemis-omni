# Rainbow Botanical Specification v1.0

## Record Contract

Every Rainbow Botanical record must be structured as a Botanical Digital Twin. It must
contain enough information to support a species page, Atlas layer, visual asset package,
social package, and future longitudinal updates.

1. Identity
2. Taxonomy
3. AI confidence
4. Scientific description
5. Morphology
6. Measurements
7. Color profile
8. Habitat
9. Geographic distribution
10. Native and naturalized range
11. Atlas integration
12. Phenology
13. Ecology

19. Cultural history
20. Linguistic and folk names
21. Literature

24. Architecture and landscape design

## Required Record Sections

### 1. Botanical Identity

- Scientific name
- Authority when known
- Common names
- Synonyms
- Taxonomic history
- AI identification candidates and confidence

### 2. Linguistic and Folk Names

- English names
- Regional names
- Indigenous names where documented and sourceable
- Historical names
- Street or colloquial names where appropriate
- Pronunciation
- Etymology

### 3. Geographic Distribution

- Native range
- Naturalized range
- Invasive or watch-list status where applicable
- USDA zone or equivalent climate-zone data
- GIS layer status
- Public-safe observation location

### 4. Historical and Cultural Context

- First recorded cultivation when known
- Introduction routes
- Cultural associations
- Traditional uses labeled as historical/traditional, not medical advice
- Literature, art, and garden history
- Mythology only where documented

### 5. Engineering and Ecology

- Root architecture
- Soil stabilization value
- Water-management behavior
- Pollinator support
- Urban resilience
- Infrastructure compatibility
- Biomimetic or computational geometry interpretation

### 6. Atlas Digital Twin

- Specimen ID
- Capture dates
- GPS privacy level
- Terrain, elevation, soil, hydrology, and solar exposure when available
- Bloom history
- Photogrammetry status
- WebGL model status
- Climate correlation status
- Longitudinal observation history

## Completeness Score

Each record should maintain a Rainbow Knowledge Score. The score is not a quality grade;
it is an operations guide showing what remains to be researched or captured.

Recommended categories:

- Scientific data
- Photography
- Historical research
- Cultural context
- Engineering analysis
- Ecology
- Atlas integration
- AI analysis
- 3D model status
- References

## Publication Rule

A specimen may be published only when:

- required identity fields are complete,
- public location is privacy-safe,
- source statuses are present,
- AI confidence is visible,
- visual assets are tied to source photos,
- the human approval gate is complete.
