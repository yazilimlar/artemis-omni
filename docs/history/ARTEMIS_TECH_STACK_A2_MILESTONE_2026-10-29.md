## Executive Record

TS-A2 is the interactive successor to TS-A1 (`/about/tech-stack.a1`, which stays unchanged). It restates
the official stack document as a working instrument and went live on 2026-10-29 after green
Typecheck · Lint · Build and Vercel preview checks, a hash-verified preview, and a squash merge.
The live file was confirmed byte-identical to the verified build (805,261 bytes).

## What shipped
- Interactive 3D Live-5D layer stack (drag / tap) and count-up verified figures.
- OctopusG constellation of the real marks: orbit view plus the 7-tier index identical to A1.
- Live 5D calculator (logic identical to A1) with base-case match badge and cost meter.
- Lifecycle time scrubber lighting concurrent phases; five-base map with live local clocks.
- Six view modes: Day, Dark (default), Executive, Matrix, Executive Light Blue, TTY; deep-link with
  `?view=day|dark|exec|matrix|exec-blue|tty`. Executive views add an Executive Brief.
- Weight cut from 9.6 MB (A1) to 0.8 MB by embedding each mark once at the right size.

## Integrity

base case (RC8.11): 186,000 LB · 850 tons · $14,143,700 direct · $16,123,818 SOV · 67 wd; P80 ×1.16.
