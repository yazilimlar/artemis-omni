The accuracy law

How this log stays the most accurate thing in the room — six rules, no exceptions.

01

Append-only

Entries are never edited or deleted. A correction is a new entry that names the entry it supersedes.

02

Atomic dual timestamps

Every entry is stamped at the moment it happens in ET and UTC at once, plus a monotonic sequence number. No back-dating.

03

SHA-256 hash chain

Each hash covers the entry plus the previous hash. Tampering with history is detectable by anyone, in seconds.

04

Time by timer only

Work time comes only from start/stop timer entries, in seconds. Message intervals are labelled as intervals — never as work time.

05

Evidence + source on everything

Every fact carries Observed / Declared / Inferred / Verified / Blocked / Stale / Unknown — and names where it came from.

06

UNKNOWN is never guessed

An unverified field literally reads UNKNOWN. A plausible guess is treated as an error, not a shortcut.
