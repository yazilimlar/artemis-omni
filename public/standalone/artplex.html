The command pictureThree ideas, in plain words

Countdown

Every pursuit shows its time to bid — live. And when a due date genuinely is not set, the board says NOT SET, in the open. A deadline is never invented to make a dashboard look complete.

Ball-in-court

Every open item names who owns the next move — us, the GC/CM, the owner, a sub or vendor, the model team, or the approver. There are no ownerless items; if nobody owns it, the board shows that, too.

Gates

A pursuit advances only through explicit gates — boundary, intake, capacity and fit, commercial, submission authorization — with hard stops. Enthusiasm is not a gate pass; evidence is.

How it worksPrinciples & engineering

Principles

  • Aggregation, never a silo. The panel reads what the projects' loops produce; it is a command view, not another place to type data.
  • Unknown is shown as unknown — a missing date, an unverified result, an unbounded estimate is labelled, not smoothed over.
  • Experience accumulates. Every bid's lessons become the next bid's starting position — that compounding is the product.
  • Names are governed. Pursuits, packages, and versions follow one naming standard, so history stays searchable forever.

Engineering notes

  • Registry-shaped data. Pursuits live in an append-only registry with evidence state and source on every entry.
  • Gated by design. ArtPlex operates inside the same private, fail-closed workspace as ArtLoop. This page is a static introduction — it connects to nothing.
  • Cost dimension, by standard. The AttemptBook ledger is the roadmap integration that gives every pursuit a comparable, evidence-classified AI cost picture.

Status & access: ArtPlex is in private operation alongside ArtLoop. There is no public demonstration and no self-service sign-up; access is by invitation only.
