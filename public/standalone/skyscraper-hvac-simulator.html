        Building the 3D model…

        Colors on the model: grey ghost = planned · red = late · blue = delivered / on the truck · orange = crane pick or install in progress · yellow = installed · teal = commissioning · green = commissioned. Schedule dates are made up to demonstrate the layer.

        Layers

        Color on pipes and ducts is temperature, exactly as on the drawing; stripe speed follows the calculated velocity. Pipe and duct sizes are exaggerated so they stay visible from far away. Weather sliders, sun, season and the 24-hour mode drive the sky, fog, snow and tower plumes.
