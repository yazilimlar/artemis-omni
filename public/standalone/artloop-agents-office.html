The Estimation Pipeline

Keep it coming!

ArtLoop carries one project from first invitation to submitted proposal inside a single governed loop. Eight senior seats work the floor, and at the center the ArtLoop core orchestrates all of it. The loop runs from the field trailer, through the main office, to headquarters. Nothing enters, changes, or leaves except through the loop, and every figure in it can be traced to its basis.

Scroll to walk the floor, or click a desk
Seat 1 of 8 · Intake + Takeoff · Field trailer

Senior MEP Engineer

The old-school read. Everything lands first in the normalized project folder archive — specs, contract documents, riders, addenda, emails and files. Then the takeoff is performed on the 2D drawings themselves, with highlights, coloring, markups, annotations, notes and descriptions on the sheets.

  • normalized archive
  • 2D takeoff
  • markups & annotations
Seat 2 of 8 · Takeoff · Main office

Senior BIM Manager

Plan views are brought together with section, profile and elevation views to build the 3D drawings. The takeoff is performed again in 3D — verified by code and geometry — consolidated into the unique quantity, and checked against the 2D takeoff.

  • plan + section + elevation
  • 3D takeoff
  • unique quantity
Seat 3 of 8 · Takeoff + Priced basis · Main office

Senior Project Engineer

Quantities and system functionality are reviewed, confirmed and verified by code. Every figure carries reference hyperlinks back to its source — contract drawings, emails, addenda, specifications, bid directives and riders.

  • code verification
  • evidence hyperlinks
  • drawings · specs · riders
Seat 4 of 8 · Priced basis · Field trailer

General Superintendent

The field read. Workload, scope details, logistics, crew size and schedule requirements are identified; installation processes, accessories, hangers, valves, bolts and nuts are counted as real scope — and the bid takeoff quantities are verified against how the work actually gets built.

  • crew & logistics
  • hangers & accessories
  • installation process
Seat 5 of 8 · Priced basis · Main office

Senior Procurement Manager

Procurement lists, long-lead items and special units are identified early. Quotes and RFPs, material and equipment pricing, and everything around them — storing, material handling, turning over — are run from this desk.

  • long-lead items
  • quotes & RFPs
  • storage & turnover
Seat 6 of 8 · Figure governance · Main office

Senior Project Manager

Reviews seats 1–5. Contract schedules, tables exported from the drawings, dates and quantities are checked; project schedule, logistics, labor estimation, trade coordination and subcontractor leveling are verified. RFIs and missing information are chased, and risks, opportunities, bottlenecks, advantages and weaknesses are identified.

  • reviews seats 1–5
  • leveling
  • RFI · risk · opportunity
Seat 7 of 8 · Figure governance · Headquarters

Project Executive

Reviews the Senior Project Manager's package at executive level: client relationship and contract posture, commercial commitments, staffing across projects, and the escalations the project team cannot carry alone. What reaches the President from this desk is an executive-ready package.

  • executive review
  • commercial posture
  • escalation
Seat 8 of 8 · Figure governance · Headquarters

President & CEO

The contractor's final internal review of seats 1–7: a quality check of the whole package, and the final assessment of risk and opportunity — taken in full view of the unverified numbers, the pending RFIs and every open component of the project.

  • reviews seats 1–7
  • quality check
  • final risk & opportunity
The Core · Not a seat

ArtLoop

By ARTEMIS Construction Intelligence. The center is not a ninth seat — it is the system itself. ArtLoop orchestrates the eight seats and collaborates with the core executors — ArtLeveler, ArtPlex, OctopusG and the other system agents — routing work desk to desk, holding the evidence chain, and finalizing the bid estimate analysis from the governed, locked version. The core routes and assembles; it originates no numbers.

  • ArtLeveler
  • ArtPlex
  • OctopusG
  • orchestration core
