02 · Four evidence classes

Amounts must retain their provenance and should not be silently blended.

ActualObserved or invoiced; source-supported
EstimatedCalculated from an explicit rate and method
DeclaredReported by a user or provider, not independently verified
SimulatedForecast or scenario, not realized spending

Aggregates with mixed source classes require visible composition; they cannot be labeled wholly actual.

03 · Technical-to-commercial reconciliation

Match usage evidence to financial records within compatible scopes and periods.

Technical ledgerRuns, retries, tokens or units, telemetry coverage
Commercial ledgerContracts, rates, invoices, credits and allocation basis
Reconciliation & varianceExplain differences · Flag gaps · Preserve unresolved items · Human review

Reconciliation does not independently prove telemetry completeness or invoice accuracy.
