Understand ArtLoop

ArtLoop connects a project's contract evidence to quantities, pricing decisions, reconciliations and a reviewable proposal. The diagrams describe the intended workflow, not a claim that every capability is deployed.

Inside the loopModules

Figure & Proposal Core

The governed ledger of the estimate: every line priced from a recorded basis, every total derived — never typed over. When a figure changes, the record shows what changed, when, and on whose authority.

Bid Version Standard

Every package carries a derived name, a SHA-256 checksum of its governed totals, and a delta log against the prior revision. Submitted versions lock; printed packages carry a QR code and checksum footer so any copy can be verified against the register.

Proposal Issuance

Print, Save, and Register are three distinct acts. Print renders a controlled document; Save downloads the artifact manifest; Register Issue is the formal act that seals a final version into the register.

Experience Capture

At bid close, a structured capture form records what the final hours actually changed — late addenda, quote movements, scope breaks — before the knowledge evaporates. The capture feeds ArtPlex, the command layer above every loop.

System Maps & 3D Views

The project drawn from its own estimate data: risers and per-level schematics generated from located quantities, read-only, with every quantity traceable to the base line it came from. The map never writes back into the estimate.

AI Assistance, Accounted

AI tools work inside the loop — document analysis, takeoff support, estimate review — under the AttemptBook standard, so the cost and provenance of every AI attempt on the project is itself part of the record.

How it worksPrinciples & engineering

Principles

  • Figures are governed, not edited. A total is a derivation, and its history is part of the deliverable.
  • Every number carries an evidence state — recorded, derived, or unknown — and unknowns are shown as unknowns.
  • Versions lock. A submitted version is frozen; corrections are new versions, never silent edits.
  • A print is a controlled document — named, checksummed, and verifiable against the register.

Engineering notes

  • Gated by design. ArtLoop runs in a private workspace protected by an owner allowlist that fails closed. This page is a static introduction — it connects to nothing.
  • Read-only bases. Views, maps, and exports read the estimate; they never write quantities into it.
  • Checksum discipline. Registers verify integrity by hash; a tampered or unsealed version is refused, not repaired.
  • One project per loop. Cross-project command lives one layer up, in ArtPlex — by design, not by omission.
