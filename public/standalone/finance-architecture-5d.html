Implementation Notes what changed

5D Tax Lab variant: control slicers are now color-grouped to match their 3D shapes and labels, tax brackets render as a stepped pyramid, and the payment satellite gains an orbit ring — building toward a 4D timeline scrubber and active 5D scenario comparison. This version replaces the pseudo-3D canvas with a real Three.js scene, uses volume-based scaling instead of radius inflation, attaches HTML info cards to geometry, implements direct drag-to-adjust for selected objects, and stores/restores scenarios locally and through URL encoding. Source constants are embedded as 2026 planning assumptions with comments in the code.
