01

The Live 5D Engineering Platform

GEOMETRY · TIME · COST · DOCUMENTS · FIELD

A 5D model is only useful if every discipline reads the same object. In the ARTEMIS platform, a duct run is simultaneously a drawn element, a calculated load path, a weighed quantity, a priced line, a scheduled activity and a field instruction. Change the geometry and quantities, labor, duration, cost, schedule of values and cashflow recalculate in the same pass — there is no export, no re-keying, and no second version of the truth to reconcile later.

L1

Geometry & 3D ModelPARAMETRIC · THREE.JS

Parametric building geometry — duct, pipe and equipment as objects with properties, not lines on a sheet. Every element carries its system, zone, floor and specification reference from the moment it is drawn.
L2

Engineering CalculationsSIZING · LOADS · TYPESET IN KATEX / LATEX

Duct and pipe sizing, airflow and pressure-drop checks, equipment loads and tonnage are computed from the geometry itself and typeset as auditable engineering mathematics — every result traceable to the formula and inputs that produced it.
L3

Quantities & Cost StructureQTO · L / M / C / S / E

Quantities are taken off the model, not measured by hand. Cost is built in the CMiC structure — Labor, Material, Construction, Subcontract, Equipment — so an estimate reads the same way in the model, the bid and the job cost ledger.
L4

Schedule & Labor Productivity4D · CREW LOADING

Hours are derived, not guessed: Hours = Quantity ÷ Productivity. Crew size and productivity rates drive duration, sequence and labor loading, so a productivity change shows up immediately as time and money — not as a surprise in the field.
L5

Specifications, RFIs & CorrespondenceDOCUMENTS ON THE OBJECT

Material specifications, submittals, RFIs and correspondence are attached to the model objects they concern. An open RFI is visible on the element it blocks; a spec section is one click from the material it governs. Nothing lives in a separate inbox.
L6

Commercial: SOV, EVM & Cashflow5D · PAYMENT · FORECAST

The schedule of values, earned value and cashflow curve are projections of the same model. Percent complete in the field moves EV, billing and forecast together — the commercial picture is the engineering picture, priced.
