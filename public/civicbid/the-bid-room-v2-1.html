What this feed covers — and what it doesn't. This board reads the NYC City Record Online (CROL) open dataset: Mayoral agencies, DDC, DEP, DOT, Parks, NYCHA, HPD and other City entities that advertise in the City Record. It does not include SCA, MTA, Port Authority, DASNY, or New York State lettings: SCA · MTA · PANYNJ · DASNY · NYS Contract Reporter. Bid documents and submission remain in PASSPort or the agency system named in each notice.

Source of truth: Every record is fetched at load time from the City of New York's open data API (dataset dg92-zbpx, City Record Online) and links to its official record at a856-cityrecord.nyc.gov. The City Record publication remains authoritative. Trade tags are CivicBid keyword tags, not official classifications. Pre-bid details are text extractions — verify in the official notice.
