A coined Artemis design language where ancient continuous-line geometry becomes a living interface system. Classical shapes remain primary; motion, depth, and material are restrained.

01 · Canon

Five classical families.

The border can move between the Greek key, running wave, palmette, bead-and-reel, and geometric mosaic. Each remains modular, vector-based, and connected at the viewport corners.

02 · Motion

Pattern becomes current.

Scroll drives the horizontal and vertical bands in opposing directions. Automatic evolution changes the active classical family as the narrative advances, while manual controls remain available.

03 · Deployment

Framing intelligence.

Auremeander is designed for Artemis dashboards, digital twins, financial models, public laboratories, and immersive engineering narratives without adding another WebGL context or image-heavy border.
