What would raise I3 → I4?

  1. Photograph involucral bracts and flower reverse.
  2. Record leaf attachment and both leaf surfaces.

Taxonomic firewall
The orange flowers may represent Cosmos sulphureus. They are comparator assets, not evidence for the active C. bipinnatus assertion.

Wind-compliant structural system

The plant reduces wind demand through low projected leaf area, flexible slender stems, and distributed branching. Motion is tolerated rather than resisted: a biological analogue for lightweight branching frames and compliant façade elements.

  • Load path: flower head → peduncle → branching nodes → primary stem → fibrous root zone.
  • Drag control: dissected leaves permit air passage.
  • Redundancy: multiple flowering branches distribute reproductive risk.
  • Next measurements: stem diameter, unsupported height, branch angles, recovery after deflection.

Ecological operating role

Late-season composite flowers provide exposed pollen and nectar resources accessible to a broad range of insects. The open plant form can operate as a pollinator corridor within mixed annual beds.

Symbiotic Cell Network
