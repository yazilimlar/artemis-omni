## What "5D" actually means

In construction and engineering, dimensions stack onto the model of record:

- **3D** — geometry (the BIM model)
- **4D** — geometry + **time** (the schedule)
- **5D** — geometry + time + **cost**

5D is where project controls becomes intelligence. Once cost is bound to scheduled
activities, you can answer the questions executives actually ask: *Are we ahead or
behind? Will we finish on budget? When will we need cash?*

<Callout title="The core idea">
A 5D model is not a bigger spreadsheet. It is a single source of truth where every
dollar is traceable to a scheduled activity and a piece of scope.
</Callout>

## From S-curve to earned value

Plot cumulative planned cost over time and you get the familiar **S-curve**. Overlay
actual cost and earned value, and three numbers fall out:

| Metric | Meaning | Healthy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CPI** | Cost performance index (earned ÷ actual) | ≥ 1.0 |
| **SPI** | Schedule performance index (earned ÷ planned) | ≥ 1.0 |
| **EAC** | Estimate at completion | ≤ budget |

When CPI drifts below 1.0, the forecast band on your cost curve widens — exactly the
signal Artemis surfaces on the homepage artifact.

## Why cash flow is the real output

Profit is an opinion; cash is a fact. A 5D model lets you project **monthly cash
flow** by combining the S-curve with payment terms and retention. That is what keeps
a project solvent — and what most teams compute far too late.
